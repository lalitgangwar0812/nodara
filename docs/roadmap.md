# Roadmap

1. **Phase 1 — Project foundation**: repository structure, local startup path, and documentation.
2. **Phase 2 — Endpoint agent**: host identification, configuration, and secure heartbeat transport.
3. **Phase 3 — Device inventory**: device registration and inventory APIs/UI.
4. **Phase 4 — Telemetry and monitoring**: ingestion, retention, and health views.
5. **Phase 5 — Alerting**: rule evaluation and alert lifecycle.
6. **Phase 6 — Incident management**: incident workflows and audit trail.
7. **Phase 7 — Controlled automation**: approved, auditable remediation execution.
8. **Phase 8 — Authentication/RBAC hardening**: identity integration and role-based access controls.
9. **Phase 9 — Docker/CI/CD**: repeatable builds, tests, and deployment pipelines.
10. **Phase 10 — Cloud deployment**: production hosting and operational configuration.
11. **Phase 11 — Observability**: logs, metrics, traces, and service-level monitoring.
